Scene

Receipt: VERIFIED

Why this is the product

Same protocol across ChatGPT-Monday (hub) and satellite mouths. “Done” is a receipt, not a sentence. Commercial use pays royalty; non-commercial stays free (see package LICENSE + COMMERCIAL.md).
